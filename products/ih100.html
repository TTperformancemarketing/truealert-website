IH Series 100

Three Ways to Call for Help at Home

A wall-mounted 4G base station paired with a water-resistant help button, worn as a pendant or wristband. Built for customers who spend most of their day at home, it lets them raise an alarm by pressing their help button, holding the base station's help button, or pulling the help cord, then talk hands-free through the base station.

  • No Phone, WiFi or Landline Needed: The base station runs on its own 4G LTE connection. Plug it in and it connects.
  • Keeps Working Through Outages: A backup battery runs the system for up to 5 hours, with alerts when AC power drops, comes back, or the battery runs low.
  • Tamper Alerts: A switch behind the mounting bracket sends an alert if the base station is pulled off the wall.
